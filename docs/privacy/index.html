Privacy

Last updated 3 September 2026

What we process

This console does not require an account. Plan parameters (model, GPU, context, concurrency) are stored in your browser’s localStorage and in the page URL so a plan can be shared. That data stays on your device unless you copy or send the URL.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, emails, payment details, or cluster credentials. There is no analytics pixel in the product UI. Hosting providers (GitHub Pages or the preview host) may log standard request metadata such as IP address and user-agent under their own policies.

KVKK / GDPR

PusulaInfra does not operate a personal-data database for this product. If you contact us via GitHub or X, the personal data in that message is processed only to respond. You may ask for deletion of that correspondence.
